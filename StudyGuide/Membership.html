Before you buy

How do I claim 50% off?

Choose Go or Pro and an available INR payment method. The checkout already uses half the regular INR price: Go is INR 750 instead of INR 1,500; Pro is INR 2,500 instead of INR 5,000. No coupon is needed. PayPal USD purchases are separately priced. Earlier purchases are not repriced.

Do I need a PIN after purchasing?

No. Log in to the account you used to pay. Your active Go or Pro membership opens its included content without a lesson PIN. Individual access codes are separate from membership.

Can I use my account on another device?

Yes. Log in with the same username and password to use your purchased access. Saved-subject and reviewed-item checkmarks remain in the browser where you created them.

What happens after I pay?

My purchases checks the payment confirmation and your current plan. If confirmation is delayed, refresh there instead of paying again. Contact support with your purchase reference if a completed payment has not activated access.

Is checkout recurring?

No. Go and Pro are one-time purchases. This checkout does not start an automatic renewal.

What if checkout is unavailable?

No payment is started. Message the KR7Tech admin on WhatsApp for assistance; direct calls may not be available. You can still explore free previews. Never send money to an unofficial account claiming to unlock KR7Tech.

How do I get help or request a refund review?

Read payment help and refunds, then contact support with your purchase reference. Never send your password or card security code.

Have an individual lesson access code?

Open the protected lesson and enter its PIN there. Each code confirmation opens only that item; it is not a Go or Pro membership.
